How this tool works: By default, the Products and Categories tabs read your current products.js right in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere until you click Download and upload the file yourself. If you've turned on instant product sync, both tabs instead read/write live to Firestore once you log in as the admin — changes (including stock levels) appear on your site within seconds. The Orders and Messages tabs always require logging in with the store's admin mobile number. This page is not linked from your main menu — keep its address private.
