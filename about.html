About Pink World

Pink World is a fashion and lifestyle store bringing you the latest in sarees, kurtis, western wear, men's collections, and intimate & innerwear — combining quality fabrics, trendy designs, and friendly in-store service. With two stores serving the Idukki district — in Erattayar and Nedumkandam — we're proud to now bring our collection online too, so you can browse and order your favourites from anywhere.
